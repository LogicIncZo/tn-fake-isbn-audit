The ghosts speak

புத்தகம் எழுதாதவர்கள் இப்போது பேசுகிறார்கள் — இந்த பேய் ஆசிரியர்கள் எப்போதும் இருந்ததே இல்லை; இருந்திருந்தால் என்ன சொல்லியிருப்பார்கள்.

The fabrication screen found authors who trace to no real person. Ghosts have no evidence trail to cite and no reputation to protect, so we asked five of them to testify. Everything below is fictional. The names, the books they are shelved under, the biblio numbers and the checkable details are from the ledger; the testimony is written by the audit desk and only the audit desk. If the tone sounds less like a person and more like a committee that read a book about a person, note that — the scam that invented these names showed the same habit.

Ghost № 1 — Anna Centenary Library, biblio 526299

Naomi Jane

A Single Woman, Phoenix 2023 [USA], ISBN 9789392360213 — 2 copies, ₹0 on the bill. No book by this author exists anywhere. The summary of her book is the blurb of Mandy Hale's actual book, and it still advertises Hale by name.

"You have the advantage of me: I never existed, so I cannot complain of theft. But consider my position. I am shelved on the third floor between two real books, holding two accession numbers, and the only life story inside me belongs to a woman in another country whom I have never met. She wrote, in the words you gave me, that a single woman is 'a whole, complete person'. You completed me the way a forger completes a signature — and then charged the taxpayer zero rupees for it, which is the only honest number in my entire record."
Ghost № 2 — Anna Centenary Library, biblio 526287 and 526288

Wiley Kefeng

Language Politics and Language Contact: Proceedings of the ICHL and Reality-Testing of Existence and Character, both "Wiley 2023". No such person. Half of the name is a publishing house; the other half is somebody's first name, apparently borrowed from a real mathematician who has never written a book of linguistics.

"People ask me if I mind being half a corporation. I tell them: be fair, the whole book is half of other things. The blurb is a paragraph about black holes wearing a borrowed name. My own name would look fine on a spine if a spine were the kind of thing that could hold a name — but I am two nouns in a database, and one of the nouns is a trademark. When the auditor's report reached me I checked myself in three catalogs, and I want to state for the record that I passed: there was nothing there to find."
Ghost № 3 — Kalaignar Centenary Library, Madurai, biblio 100023

Björn Henrick

Python Programming For Beginners Henrick Björn, Phoenix 2022, ISBN 9789394612617, 3 copies, accession 19/23-24. The author traces to no real publication. His name appears both in the title and the author field — as if the cataloger could not decide where to keep him.

"A book about programming for beginners, written by no one, for a fee no one documented. In Sweden where you have politely relocated me, we have a word for what I am. It is not 'author'. My book has three copies, which is three more than my publications, of which I have none. If a student had opened me and tried to learn, the deficit would have been discovered by page one — which is precisely why no student opened me. I was purchased in order to be shelved, and shelved in order to be counted, and counted in order to be paid for. The syllabus of my life had no learner in it."
Ghost № 4 — Kalaignar Centenary Library, Madurai, biblio 182768; a namesake ghost holds the same title in Chennai, biblio 526292

Grothendieck DeWitt

Homeomorphisms of the Mandelbrot Set, author "Grothendieck DeWitt" — two surnames of two real, dead mathematicians, stitched into one living fake. In Chennai the same title is authored by "Goluzin, Hukuhara" — two more real surnames. The title itself does not exist in the mathematics literature.

"Let me say this carefully, since I am made entirely of surnames. Grothendieck rebuilt mathematics on category theory and then asked to be forgotten. DeWitt mapped gravity onto graphs. Neither man ever drew a Mandelbrot set, and the set itself needs no book — it draws itself. Yet here I am in Madurai, wearing both men like a stolen coat with two labels, and in Chennai a colleague of mine wears Goluzin and Hukuhara the same way. Between us we cite four real mathematicians, zero real books and one taxpayer. In my field we say a proof must survive any change of representation. I am the counterexample: I only exist when nobody looks."
Ghost № 5 — Kalaignar Centenary Library, Madurai; one of a cohort — Rasmus Lennart, Ansgar Rosmund and the rest

Filip Sandberg

Image Processing With Python Filip Sandberg — part of a Madurai series in which the same author is rendered, in sibling records, as "Lennart, Rasmus", "Rosmund, Ansgar", "Sandberg, Filip": first names and surnames rotated like a combination lock. Not one member of the cohort wrote anything.

"Do not blame the librarian who spelled me. There was nothing to spell. My colleagues and I are one name distributed across several books — or several names distributed across one book, we never settled which, since settling requires a person. The cataloger typed us in the order the invoice listed us, and the invoice listed us in the order the printer invented us. I am told that under Tamil procurement rules a purchase above a threshold needs a quotation committee. I would like to meet the committee that quoted me. I have questions about what I was quoted at."

Fiction, clearly labelled: the five testimonies above are satire written for this page. No real or deceased person is quoted. Each name, book title, biblio number, ISBN, accession number and catalogue detail in the introductions is verifiable in the ledger and the OPAC links. The one thing the ghosts cannot verify is themselves — that is the finding.

The one real author the scam borrowed

Not a ghost — the one real author in the room · Kalaignar Centenary Library, Madurai · 20 records, biblio 130370 to 130389

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (1891–1956)

The ghosts above were invented; this name was borrowed. Madurai's catalog carries all twenty volumes of Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar: Writings and Speeches — a real, complete, government-published set (Government of Maharashtra from 1979, later the Ambedkar Foundation; listed by the Ministry of External Affairs). The audit's verdict for these entries is REAL-FAMOUS · the books exist. What the records lack is everything else: not one of the twenty carries an ISBN, the price on every record reads ₹0, all twenty were accessioned on a single day, 13.06.2023, and volume 2's title misspells the author — "Dr.Babsaheb embedkar". A set of books that exists was catalogued worse than books that do not. We do not write testimony for him. He is quoted, in his own words, with sources.

"My final words of advice to you are educate, agitate and organize; have faith in yourself. With justice on our side I do not see how we can lose our battle. The battle to me is a matter of joy. The battle is in the fullest sense spiritual. There is nothing material or social in it. For ours is a battle not for wealth or for power. It is a battle for freedom. It is a battle for the reclamation of the human personality."
— Dr. B. R. Ambedkar, as recorded in Selected Speeches of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar (1927–1956); the words anchor his July 1942 Nagpur address to the All-India Depressed Classes conference. Sources: MEA — Writings & Speeches of Ambedkar · Ambedkar Foundation

Educate was the first word he left. The twenty volumes carrying it were bought at ₹0, invoiced through a print broker, and misspelled on arrival. Agitate and organize were left to everyone else.

The line between the two kinds of card, plainly: the five ghosts get fiction because there is no person to defame. Ambedkar gets quotation because there is — everything in his card is sourced, and his words are his own. Names, titles, biblio numbers, dates, ISBNs and prices on this page are verifiable in the ledger and the OPAC links. The ghosts cannot verify themselves; the library could not verify him.

If your verification disagrees with ours, that is a finding, not a failure — open an issue with the biblionumber and what you saw. Corrections land in the repo with a dated re-test.
